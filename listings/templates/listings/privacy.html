2. Collecting and Using Your Personal Information

2.1 Types of Data Collected

2.1.1 Personal Data

You do not need an account and do not have to give Us any personal information to use the Service. If You email Us, We receive Your email address and the contents of Your message.

2.1.2 Usage Data

Usage Data is collected automatically when You use the Service. Our hosting provider records it in server logs. It includes Your Device's Internet Protocol address (IP address), browser type and version, the pages of Our Service that You visit, and the time and date of Your visit.

2.1.3 Cookies

We do not use tracking, analytics or advertising cookies. The Service may set a technical cookie that it needs to work, such as a security token. You can block Cookies in Your browser settings, but parts of the Service may stop working.

2.2 Use of Your Personal Data

The Company may use Personal Data for the following purposes:

  • To provide and maintain Our Service, including to monitor its use and keep it secure.

  • To manage Your requests: To attend and manage Your requests to Us. If You email Us, We use Your email address and message only to reply to You. We do not add it to a mailing list.

We do not sell Personal Data. We may share Your Personal Data in the following situations:

  • With Service Providers: Our hosting provider, Render, hosts the Service. It stores Our database and records server logs.
  • With Your consent: We may disclose Your Personal Data for any other purpose with Your consent.

2.3 Retention of Your Personal Data

The Company will retain Your Personal Data only for as long as is necessary for the purposes set out in this Privacy Policy. We will retain and use Your Personal Data to the extent necessary to comply with Our legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce Our legal agreements and policies.

  • Server logs are kept by Our hosting provider under its own log settings.
  • Emails to Us are kept for as long as We need to answer You and handle any follow-up.
  • Agent IDs and listing text from public listings stay in Our database until We remove them, for example after a request from the person concerned.

When We no longer need Personal Data, We delete it. Copies may remain in backups until they are overwritten.

2.4 Transfer of Your Personal Data

Our hosting provider runs the Service from servers in Singapore, so Your information is processed outside Vietnam. Where You are located, the data protection laws may differ from those of Singapore or Vietnam.

Where required by applicable law, We will ensure that international transfers of Your Personal Data are subject to appropriate safeguards. The Company will take reasonable steps to make sure Your data is treated securely and in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

2.5 Delete Your Personal Data

You have the right to ask Us to give You access to, correct or delete the Personal Data We hold about You. To make a request, email saipricesupport@gmail.com.

We may need to keep certain information when We have a legal obligation or lawful basis to do so.

2.6 Disclosure of Your Personal Data

2.6.1 Law Enforcement

Under certain circumstances, the Company may disclose Your Personal Data if required to do so by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities (e.g. a court or a government agency).

2.6.2 Other Legal Requirements

The Company may disclose Your Personal Data in the good-faith belief that such action is necessary to:

  • Comply with a legal obligation

  • Protect the personal safety of Users of the Service or the public

2.7 Security of Your Personal Data

The security of Your Personal Data is important to Us, but remember that no method of transmission over the Internet, or method of electronic storage, is 100% secure. While We strive to use commercially reasonable means to protect Your Personal Data, We cannot guarantee its absolute security.

3. Listing Data and Agent Information

The Service shows property listings that We collect from public real estate websites. We do not store agent or seller names or phone numbers, and the Service shows no agent details.

We do store an internal agent ID taken from the source listing. It is a number only, and Our database does not link it to a name or contact details. The listing text We store can contain contact details if the poster typed them into the description, so We try to remove phone numbers from listing titles, addresses and descriptions before storing them. We collect this information from public pages and not from the people themselves, and We use it only to run the Service. We do not sell it and do not use it to contact anyone.

If You are an agent or seller and want a listing or Your agent ID removed, email saipricesupport@gmail.com with a link to Your listing. We review each request and delete what We hold about You where We can.

4. Children's and Minors' Privacy

The Service is not directed to, and We do not knowingly collect Personal Information from, anyone under the age of 18.

If You are a parent or guardian and You believe Your child has provided Us with Personal Information, please contact Us. If We become aware that We have collected Personal Information from anyone under the age of 18, We will take steps to remove that information from Our servers as soon as reasonably possible.

We have no control over and assume no responsibility for the content, privacy policies or practices of any third-party sites or services.

6. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update Our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the new version on this page and update the "Last updated" date at the top. For material changes, We will also put a notice on the Service before the change takes effect.

You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes. Changes to this Privacy Policy are effective when they are posted on this page.
