9. Asking Prices, Predictions and Flags

The Service shows asking prices taken from public property listings. An asking price is what a seller or agent wants, not what a buyer paid. We do not have sale prices and do not know what any property is worth.

A predicted price is a statistical estimate of a typical asking price. It can be wrong by 20 percent or more. It is not a valuation or an appraisal, and it is not financial, legal or real estate advice.

A flag marks a listing that matches one of Our automated rules, such as an asking price far below the predicted price or a long time on the market. A flag does not mean a listing is false, misleading or fraudulent, and it says nothing about the person who posted it. Check the original listing and get professional advice before You act on anything shown on the Service.
